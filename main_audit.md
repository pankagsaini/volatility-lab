## 1. Executive Assessment & System Architecture Overview

Volatility Lab is an institutional-grade quantitative volatility research, live market monitoring, and multi-leg derivatives backtesting workstation engineered for Indian equity index derivatives (**NIFTY 50** and **NIFTY BANK**) on the National Stock Exchange of India (NSE). The platform integrates a serverless Google Apps Script cloud collector, a local Google Drive desktop mirror sync engine, a 5-year 1-minute historical options and futures SQLite/gzip archive (~204.88 million minute candle rows across 91,785+ completed jobs), a statutory dated brokerage/tax calculator, and interactive visualization interfaces.

Across all 12 root files, 71 files and 5 subdirectories inside [`Volatility Lab/`](https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/tree/master/Volatility%20Lab), and the live Google Drive Apps Script deployment, the codebase demonstrates strong foundational engineering discipline: strict point-in-time (`AvailableAt`) lookahead guards in [backtest\_signals.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/backtest_signals.py) and [har\_research.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/har_research.py), refusal to fabricate historical implied volatility or bid-ask spreads from OHLC candles ([IV DATA POLICY.md](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/IV%2520DATA%2520POLICY.md)), dated NSE contract lot sizes, and 4-regime statutory fee tables in [broker\_charges.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/broker_charges.py).

## 2. Quantitative Mathematics, Volatility Surface & Forecasting Audit

### 2.1 Critical Mathematical & Quantitative Defects

1. **Critical Session-to-Calendar Variance Bridge Distortion in [expiry\_forecast.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/expiry_forecast.py) (`expiry_forecast`, lines 539, 574–583)**:
   * **Mechanism**: In [`vol_lib.py`](https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%20Lab/vol_lib.py) and [forecast.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/forecast.py), daily variance $\sigma_{	ext{day}}^2$ is estimated over trading sessions and annualized by $245$ sessions/year ($\sigma_{	ext{ann}}^2 = 245 \cdot \sigma_{	ext{day}}^2$). In [expiry\_forecast.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/expiry_forecast.py), the code recovers per-session variance via `ewma = rv_snapshot(training)['ewma_next_session_vol']**2 / 245` and `variances = (path['Daily conditional vol %'].to_numpy()/100)**2 / 245`, sums over $N_{	ext{sessions}} = 	ext{weights.sum()}$, and then divides by calendar years `years = (end - now).total_seconds() / (365 * 86400)` ($D_{	ext{cal}} / 365$):
     ```python
     years = (end-now).total_seconds()/(365*86400)
     ewma = rv_snapshot(training)['ewma_next_session_vol']**2/245
     result['EWMA expiry RV'] = float(100*np.sqrt(ewma*weights.sum()/years))
     result['GARCH expiry RV'] = float(100*np.sqrt(np.dot(variances,weights)/years))
     ```
   * **Quantitative Impact**: Dividing cumulative trading-session variance by calendar years $D_{	ext{cal}} / 365$ instead of trading years $N_{	ext{sessions}} / 245$ multiplies true annualized volatility by $\sqrt{rac{365}{245} \cdot rac{N_{	ext{sessions}}}{D_{	ext{cal}}}}$. On a Monday close for a Friday expiry ($N_{	ext{sessions}}=4, D_{	ext{cal}}=4$), forecasted RV is **overstated by +22.06%** ($\sqrt{365/245} = 1.2206$). On a Friday close for a Monday expiry ($N_{	ext{sessions}}=1, D_{	ext{cal}}=3$), forecasted RV is **crushed by -29.53%** ($\sqrt{365 / (245 	imes 3)} = 0.7047$).
   * **Solution**: Annualize the weighted mean session variance consistently on the 245-session trading clock:
     ```python
     mean_session_var_ewma = ewma
     mean_session_var_garch = float(np.dot(variances, weights) / weights.sum())
     result['EWMA expiry RV'] = float(100.0 * np.sqrt(mean_session_var_ewma * 245.0))
     result['GARCH expiry RV'] = float(100.0 * np.sqrt(mean_session_var_garch * 245.0))
     ```

2. **Silent Broken Call Signature in [relative\_value.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/relative_value.py) (`observe`, lines 68–73)**:
   * **Mechanism**: `observe()` calls `matched = expiry_forecast(quote, history_frame)` with only 2 arguments in reversed order, whereas `expiry_forecast(frame, expiry, as_of, iv=None)` in [expiry\_forecast.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/expiry_forecast.py) requires 3 positional arguments `(frame, expiry, as_of)`. Furthermore, `observe()` looks up `matched.get('Forecast')`, which does not exist in `expiry_forecast`'s output dict (`'GARCH expiry RV'`, `'EWMA expiry RV'`). Because lines 68–73 catch `(OSError, ValueError, KeyError, TypeError)`, this `TypeError` is silently swallowed on every observation.
   * **Solution**: Call `matched = expiry_forecast(history_frame, quote['Expiry'], quote['As of'], quote.get('IV'))` and extract `matched.get('GARCH expiry RV')` and `matched.get('EWMA expiry RV')`.

3. **Hardcoded `2026` Year Boundary Crash in [expiry\_forecast.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/expiry_forecast.py) (`sessions`, line 803)**:
   * **Mechanism**: `if start.year != 2026 or end.year != 2026: raise ValueError('Verified trading calendar currently covers 2026 only.')` crashes on any late-2026 option expiring in January/March 2027, any early-2026 check where the last trading day was `2025-12-31`, and even on `2026-12-31` itself when `elapsed = sessions(last + timedelta(days=1), cutoff)` passes `start = 2027-01-01 > end = 2026-12-31`.
   * **Solution**: Add `if start > end: return []` as the first line of `sessions(start, end)` and validate against dynamic supported years in `market_calendar.py`.

4. **Conflicting ATM IV & 25-Delta Butterfly Definitions Across [option\_math.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/option_math.py) and [smile.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/smile.py)**:
   * **Mechanism**: `skew_metrics(chain)` in [option\_math.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/option_math.py) defines the ATM anchor as `0.5 * (delta_iv(chain, 'PE', 0.5) + delta_iv(chain, 'CE', 0.5))` (50-delta proxy), whereas `smile_points(chain, strikes, spot)` in [smile.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/smile.py) defines ATM as the average of Call and Put IV at the listed strike closest to spot ($K pprox S$). Because positive carry makes spot call delta $> 0.50$ at $K = S$, the two modules report conflicting ATM IV and 25D Butterfly values for the same chain. Additionally, [option\_math.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/option_math.py) lacks an arbitrage-bounded BSM implied volatility root-finder (`scipy.optimize.brentq`) and analytical higher-order Greeks (`gamma`, `vega`, `theta`, `vanna`, `volga`).

5. **Missing Forward Implied Volatility & Calendar Arbitrage Flag in [term\_structure.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/term_structure.py)**:
   * **Mechanism**: `term_structure(rows, symbol)` computes only the level spread `following['IV'] - current['IV']` and raises `ValueError('Duplicate expiry in snapshot.')` if duplicate rows exist. It omits forward implied volatility $\sigma_{	ext{fwd}}(T_1, T_2) = \sqrt{rac{\sigma_2^2 T_2 - \sigma_1^2 T_1}{T_2 - T_1}}$ and calendar-arbitrage detection ($\sigma_2^2 T_2 < \sigma_1^2 T_1$).

6. **Additional Quantitative Edge Cases**:
   * **NSE Market Close Timestamp (`15:40` vs. `15:30` IST)**: [expiry\_forecast.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/expiry_forecast.py) uses `time(15, 40)` not only as a post-close data-availability cutoff but also as `closing = datetime.combine(date, time(15, 40), tzinfo=IST)` for intraday session fraction weighting (`385` minutes instead of NSE's actual `375`-minute session `09:15–15:30 IST`).
   * **Synthetic Future Discounting ([synthetic\_future.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/synthetic_future.py))**: `calculate()` computes `synth = stk + c - p` using raw `ltp` rather than bid-ask midpoints and omits the discount factor $e^{rT}$ in $F = K + e^{rT}(C - P)$.
   * **3D Vol Surface DTE & Skew Label Bug ([vol\_surface\_3d.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/vol_surface_3d.py))**: `load_expiry_chains` computes DTE against wall-clock `datetime.now(IST).date()` instead of the snapshot's saved `valuation` timestamp; `extract_surface_mesh` hardcodes `strike_step = 50.0 if ref_spot < 35000 else 100.0`; and `front_skew = float(z_matrix[0, left_idx] - z_matrix[0, right_idx])` at array indices `0.2 * len` and `0.8 * len` is labeled `"Front Skew (96%-104%)"` even when the user changes `moneyness_range_pct` away from `8.0%`.
   * **Missing Range Estimators & EWMA Cold-Start Bias ([`vol_lib.py`](https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%20Lab/vol_lib.py))**: Parkinson (1980) and Garman-Klass (1980) estimators are absent, Rogers-Satchell is computed internally but not exposed, and `rv_snapshot` initializes `ewma_var = float(returns.iloc[0]**2)` from a single day's squared return instead of a warm-start sample variance mean.

## 3. Cloud Capture, Upstox Ingestion & Google Drive Sync Audit

### 3.1 Code Drift Between GitHub (`cloud/apps_script/Code.gs`) and Live Google Drive (`Volatility Lab - Upstox Capture`)

A direct line-by-line inspection of your live Google Drive Apps Script ([Volatility Lab - Upstox Capture](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://script.google.com/d/1_nNJrwJaGMmtctFJYhZudef3rCEtRiZRM9fZqn8BHnI-fnAMupLNqk-A/edit%3Fusp%3Ddrivesdk), 573 lines, modified November 5, 2026) against [Volatility Lab/cloud/apps\_script/Code.gs](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/cloud/apps_script/Code.gs) (~887 lines) reveals major production drift:

1. **`failureWatchdog()` and `weeklyCleanup()` Deleted in Live Drive `Code.gs`**:
   * The GitHub version defines `failureWatchdog()` (to create/resolve Google Calendar alerts) and `weeklyCleanup()` (to purge Drive files older than 14 days after local receipt verification).
   * In the live Google Drive script, both functions were completely removed when switching to 1-minute snapshots on your 5TB Drive plan, yet `installIntradaySchedule()` on line 326 still filters `t.getHandlerFunction() === 'weeklyCleanup'`, and [`Repair Cloud Sync.ps1`](https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%20Lab/Repair%20Cloud%20Sync.ps1) still enforces the 8-day/14-day receipt check (`google_archive_sync.py --verify-receipt`).
2. **Weekend Execution of `reconcilePriorSession()` (`Code.gs`, lines 351–359)**:
   * In `scheduledCapture()`, the pre-market reconciliation window check (`if (mins >= 420 && mins < 540) { reconcilePriorSession(); return; }`) is placed **above** the weekend guard (`if (weekday > 5) { return; }`). As a result, `reconcilePriorSession()` executes every minute between 07:00 and 09:00 IST on Saturdays and Sundays.
3. **Artificial Runtime Budget Inflation & 90-Minute Quota Exhaustion (`Code.gs`, lines 405–448)**:
   * `scheduledCapture()` runs every 1 minute from 09:00 to 16:00 IST (420 triggers/day) plus EOD and reconciliation windows. In the `finally` block (line 446), `b.ms += Date.now() - started + 1000;` adds an artificial `+1000 ms` penalty to every single execution—burning **7 full minutes** of runtime budget per day on padding alone and causing the 75-minute cap (`budget.ms >= 75 * 60000`) to halt live capture before the 15:30 IST market close on busy days.
4. **HTTP 401/403 Retry Storm on Expired `UPSTOX_TOKEN` (`Code.gs`, lines 211–221)**:
   * Upstox access tokens expire daily at 03:30 IST. When `UPSTOX_TOKEN` expires, `apiAll_(requests)` receives `Broker HTTP 401` on all ~15 batch requests, and the fallback loop (`if (res.error) { data = api_(res.path, res.params); }`) immediately retries every single request sequentially, doubling failed calls and burning runtime budget.
5. **Unauthenticated `doGet(e)` Web App Exposure (`appsscript.json` & `Code.gs`, lines 513–572)**:
   * `appsscript.json` sets `"access": "ANYONE_ANONYMOUS"`, and `doGet(e)` serves raw market snapshots and internal `ScriptProperties` without checking an API key parameter. Furthermore, when `LATEST_SNAPSHOT_FILE_ID` is absent, `doGet(e)` scans only the first 60 files returned by `folder.getFiles()` (`while (files.hasNext() && count < 60)`), which is not ordered newest-first in Google Drive and returns stale snapshots.

### 3.2 Background Downloader Hangs, `"running"` Lockouts & Rate-Limit Collisions

1. **Critical Cache Crash (`KeyError: 'data'`) in [futures\_backtest\_download.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/futures_backtest_download.py) (`fetch`, lines 12–20)**:
   * On line 18, `fetch()` saves new archives as `body = {'provider': provider, 'path': path, 'params': params, 'meta': meta, 'retrieved_at': ...}` **without including `'data': data`**. On the next run, when `if file.exists():` is true on line 15, `return json.load(f)['data']` immediately crashes with `KeyError: 'data'`.
   * Additionally, line 31 calls `/v2/historical-candle/intraday/` (which is Upstox's current-day-only endpoint) with spot `INDICES` keys instead of dated futures instrument keys.
2. **Why `backtest_download.py` and `futures_backtest_download.py` Get Stuck in `"running"` Status**:
   * In [backtest\_download.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/backtest_download.py) (lines 116–117), `status(c, 'running', current=...)` is written to `status.json` immediately before `data = future.result()`. If the console window is closed, paused by Windows QuickEdit mode, or killed via `TerminateProcess` (`KeyboardInterrupt` / `SystemExit` inherit from `BaseException`, not `Exception`), the `except Exception:` block never runs and `status.json` remains permanently stuck at `"status": "running"`.
   * Furthermore, when a single job fails 3 times in 45 seconds (`attempts >= 2` on line 141, because `next_jobs()` always picks `ORDER BY rowid LIMIT 1`), `backtest_download.py` sets `paused_error` and **terminates the entire worker loop (`return`)**, halting all 4,900+ remaining pending jobs.
3. **Why [background\_refresh.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/background_refresh.py) Runs Non-Stop Redundant Polling Loops**:
   * In [background\_refresh.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/background_refresh.py), `is_stale(state, key)` unconditionally returns `True`, while `status()` runs an `@st.fragment(run_every=2)` loop every 2 seconds. Moreover, both `take()` and `poll()` call `_collect()`, which executes `del state[slot]` non-atomically (risking `KeyError` race conditions).
4. **Cross-Process SQLite Lock Contention in [google\_archive\_sync.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/google_archive_sync.py)**:
   * The Windows Scheduled Task (`Repair Cloud Sync.ps1`) runs `google_archive_sync.py --loop` every 10 minutes in a separate OS process while the Streamlit app also triggers `ensure_sync()`. Because `_guard = threading.Lock()` only synchronizes threads inside a single Python process, concurrent OS processes collide on `data/google_archive/monitor.sqlite` unless wrapped in `collector_support.data_lock` with `PRAGMA journal_mode=WAL; PRAGMA busy_timeout=30000;`.

## 4. Options & Futures Backtesting Engines & Execution Realism Audit

### 4.1 Critical Backtest Engine Bugs & Bottlenecks

1. **Positional Argument Mismatch in [backtest\_jobs.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/backtest_jobs.py) (`_options`) Calling [strategy\_backtest.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/strategy_backtest.py) (`simulate`)**:
   * **Bug**: `backtest_jobs._options(task)` calls `simulate(contracts, series, INDICES[symbol], settings, signals)` (5 positional arguments, with `contracts` first and `settings` fourth). However, `strategy_backtest.simulate(s, contracts, ticks, signals, callback=None)` expects the settings dictionary `s` as the **first** argument (`s, contracts, ticks, signals`).
   * **Solution**: Update `backtest_jobs._options(task)` to call `simulate(settings, contracts, series, signals)`.

2. **Intraday Missing-Bar Skip Creates Survivorship Bias in [strategy\_backtest.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/strategy_backtest.py) (`simulate`, lines 140–171)**:
   * **Bug**: During minute-by-minute stop/target monitoring (`while cursor < exit_time`), if any option leg has a single 1-minute bar without trades (`not valid_price(b, 3)`), `simulate()` calls `record_skip(skips, day, 'Missing monitoring minute; risk path unknown')` and completely erases the trade—even though the trade already entered at `09:20`! Discarding entered trades whenever an OTM wing has a quiet minute introduces survivorship bias.
   * **Solution**: Carry forward the last known close price (`last_valid_close`) for non-fill mark-to-market monitoring checks while still requiring a valid traded bar (`volume > 0`) for the actual exit fill (advancing to the next traded minute if the trigger minute's next open has zero volume).

3. **375x Per-Day Redundant Fee Calculation Inside the 1-Minute Monitoring Loop ([strategy\_backtest.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/strategy_backtest.py))**:
   * **Bottleneck**: Inside the `while cursor < exit_time` loop (up to 375 minutes per session), `simulate()` calls `broker_charges.charge()` for every leg at every single minute just to check `net <= -s['stop']` or `net >= s['target']`.
   * **Solution**: Pre-calculate entry charges once at trade entry and use a fast inline linear multiplier for exit charges during monitoring, invoking the full `broker_charges.charge()` breakdown only once when the exit is actually triggered.

4. **Uncached Gzip Decompression in [backtest\_engine.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/backtest_engine.py) (`load`) & Duplicate Simulation Logic**:
   * **Bottleneck**: Unlike [futures\_backtest.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/futures_backtest.py) (which uses a thread-safe `OrderedDict` LRU cache `_FILE_CACHE` capped at 2,048 files / 75,000 rows), `backtest_engine.load()` unzips `.json.gz` files from scratch on every run. Furthermore, `backtest_engine.simulate()` duplicates the prior-minute close lookup, slippage math, and fee aggregation of `strategy_backtest.simulate()`.

5. **SPAN Margin Floor & Synchronous Fee Scraping ([strategy\_backtest.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/strategy_backtest.py), [broker\_charges.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/broker_charges.py), [backtest\_jobs.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/backtest_jobs.py))**:
   * `estimate_exchange_margin()` evaluates intrinsic payoff across `[-15%, -psr, -psr/2, 0, +psr/2, +psr, +15%]` plus strikes, underestimating margin on short strangles outside $\pm 15\%$ because it lacks an NSE Short Option Minimum (SOM, ~5% of underlying notional) floor and a defined-risk Iron Condor spread margin floor (~1.5%–2% of notional).
   * In [backtest\_jobs.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/backtest_jobs.py), `fee_status()` and `inventory_status()` share the same `_fee_lock`, so a 12-second HTTP timeout when `broker_charges.refresh()` scrapes `https://upstox.com/brokerage-charges/` freezes inventory status checks in the UI.

## 5. UI/UX Appearance, Streamlit Cloud & FastAPI Web Server Audit

### 5.1 Visual, CSS, and Streamlit Cloud Defects

1. **Invalid CSS Hex (`#20e42`) and Broken Radio Selector (`div[div[...]`) in [dashboard.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/dashboard.py) (lines 85–112)**:
   * `[data-testid="stPlotlyChart"] { border: 1px solid #20e42; ... }` uses a 5-digit hex code (`#20e42` instead of `#243348`), so browsers drop the border on all Plotly charts.
   * `div[div[data-testid="stRadio"] label:has(input:checked)` has a doubled unclosed `[` bracket, preventing selected radio buttons from showing their emerald highlight.
   * Market status badges (`NSE LIVE`, `PRE-OPEN`, `CLOSED`) use glaring light-mode pastel backgrounds (`#ecfdf5`, `#fffbe6`, `#f3f4f6`) against the `#0c1321` dark theme.
2. **Per-Rerun `compile()` + `exec()` Overhead in [`streamlit_app.py`](https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/streamlit_app.py) & Single-Load Bug in [app.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/app.py)**:
   * Because the subfolder is named `Volatility Lab/` (with a space), `streamlit_app.py` reads `dashboard.py` from disk and runs `compile()` + `exec()` on every single 2-second fragment rerun. Meanwhile, `app.py` (`import streamlit_app`) goes blank on its second rerun because Python does not re-run cached module imports.
3. **UTC vs. IST Timezone Bug on Streamlit Cloud in [refresh\_policy.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/refresh_policy.py) (`refresh_due`)**:
   * `refresh_due(now, result, elapsed)` checks `now.hour * 60 + now.minute` against IST market minutes (`555` to `940`) without first converting `now` to `ZoneInfo("Asia/Kolkata")`. On Streamlit Community Cloud (where container system time is UTC), the app misinterprets UTC hours as IST and suppresses live refreshes during the morning NSE trading session!
4. **Missing `folderWatchBlacklist` in Root [.streamlit/config.toml](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/.streamlit/config.toml)**:
   * `folderWatchBlacklist = [".runtime", ".dependencies", "data", "logs", "__pycache__", ".auth"]` is present in `Volatility Lab/.streamlit/config.toml` but missing from the root `.streamlit/config.toml`, causing SQLite/JSON writes in `data/` to trigger full Streamlit file-watcher reruns.
5. **Three Parallel UI Stacks & Hardcoded `.dependencies` Path in [web\_server.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/web_server.py)**:
   * Term Structure, Skew, and 3D Vol Surface are implemented three separate times across [monitor\_ui.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/monitor_ui.py), [restored\_lab.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/restored_lab.py), and [web\_data.py](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/blob/master/Volatility%2520Lab/web_data.py) + [frontend/](https://www.google.com/search?q=https://github.com/pankagsaini/volatility-lab/tree/master/Volatility%2520Lab/frontend).
   * `web_server.py` hardcodes `LOCAL_PLOTLY = ROOT / '.dependencies/plotly/package_data/plotly.min.js'`, which breaks outside the local Windows `.dependencies` bundle.

## 6. Prioritized Remediation Roadmap & Concrete Code Fixes

### Priority 1 — Immediate Bug & Data-Integrity Fixes (Zero Architectural Disruption)

2. **Fix `relative_value.py:observe()` & `backtest_jobs.py:_options()` Call Signatures**: Pass `(history_frame, quote['Expiry'], quote['As of'], quote.get('IV'))` to `expiry_forecast()` in `relative_value.py`, and pass `(settings, contracts, series, signals)` to `strategy_backtest.simulate()` in `backtest_jobs.py`.
3. **Fix `futures_backtest_download.py` Cache Crash & `backtest_download.py` 3-Strike Exit**: Include `'data': data` in the saved JSON payload on line 18 of `futures_backtest_download.py`; in `backtest_download.py`, catch `BaseException` to update `status.json` cleanly on exit and mark individual 3-strike jobs as `'failed'` (`UPDATE jobs SET state='failed'`) while continuing the worker loop for the remaining pending jobs.
4. **Fix `refresh_policy.py` IST Conversion & `background_refresh.py` Staleness Loop**: Convert `now` to `ZoneInfo('Asia/Kolkata')` at the top of `refresh_due()`, implement a monotonic TTL check in `background_refresh.is_stale()`, and use `state.pop(slot, None)` in `_collect()`.
5. **Sync Live Drive `Code.gs` with GitHub `cloud/apps_script/Code.gs`**: Move `if (weekday > 5) return;` above `reconcilePriorSession()`, remove the `+ 1000` ms runtime budget padding, skip sequential retries on HTTP `401`/`403`, and protect `doGet(e)` with a `WEBHOOK_KEY` Script Property.

### Priority 2 — UI/UX Polish & Streamlit Cloud Optimization
1. **Repair `dashboard.py` CSS & Entrypoint**: Fix `#20e42` -> `#243348` and `div[data-testid="stRadio"] label:has(input:checked)`, switch session pills to dark-mode translucent surfaces, wrap `dashboard.py` in `def main():`, call `dashboard.main()` directly from `streamlit_app.py` and `app.py` (eliminating `exec()`), and copy `folderWatchBlacklist` into the root `.streamlit/config.toml`.
2. **Enhance Plotly Ergonomics in `monitor_ui.py`, `term_structure.py`, and `vol_surface_3d.py`**: Add Forward Implied Volatility $\sigma_{	ext{fwd}}(T_1, T_2)$ and calendar-arbitrage flags to `term_structure.py`; plot DTE proportionally on the x-axis with a secondary axis for Synthetic Futures basis; replace crowded `mode='lines+markers+text'` static labels on the smile chart with clean hover templates; and interpolate front skew in `vol_surface_3d.py` at exact `96%` and `104%` moneyness.

### Priority 3 — Code Consolidation & Vectorization
1. **Unify Math & Realized Volatility Modules**: Consolidate `rolling_rv` (`forecast.py`) and `rv_snapshot` (`vol_lib.py`) into a single vectorized `rolling_rv_suite()` in `vol_lib.py` (adding Parkinson, Garman-Klass, and Rogers-Satchell, plus vectorized `.ewm()`), and add a `scipy.optimize.brentq` IV solver and full analytical Greeks (`bsm_greeks`) to `option_math.py`.
2. **Speed Up 1-Minute Options Backtests**: Move per-leg `broker_charges.charge()` out of the 375-minute intraday monitoring loop in `strategy_backtest.py`, carry forward `last_valid_close` during non-fill monitoring minutes, and share the `_FILE_CACHE` LRU mechanism from `futures_backtest.py` with the options loader.
