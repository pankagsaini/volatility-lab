## Pricing, time and units

- **Forward.** The Python implementation at volatility_lab/pricing/option_math.py:215 and choice at volatility_lab/pricing/pricing_policy.py:82 use F = K + exp(rT)(C − P). The browser's selector is at docs/index.html:1543. Priority is a timestamped matching listed future, then qualifying ATM option mids with both spreads strictly below 3% of midpoint, then spot BSM with verified rate and index dividend inputs. Missing valid inputs suppress the model. See scripts/test_pricing_policy.cjs, and docs/pricing-inputs.json:14 and :26 for the dated NSE yield proxy and RBI 91-day bill convention. These inputs do not measure an individual contract's future dividends. The quoted ATM spread and the ability to execute all strategy legs are separate checks.
- **Expiry clock and roll.** Baseline docs/index.html:1596 imposes one minute of remaining time even at expiry, and :1613 rolls at session end. The patch makes both clocks zero exactly at expiry, uses the expiring contract cutoff for 0DTE, and rolls the default selection at 15:30 IST on the prior regular trading session (at the announced close on a special session). A manual expiry selection is preserved by the catalog refresh. Tests in scripts/test_frontend.cjs at the 15:29:59/15:30 boundary and Dec 29 close cover these distinctions. The calendar clock drives pricing. The volatility clock is a convention of remaining minutes divided by 245 × 375 and a diagnostic, not an independent calibrated forecast. At 14:05 on 17 November the fixture has 95 minutes until the dated derivatives close, or 95/525600 calendar years.
- **Session vs final settlement.** NSE's applicable August–November regular derivatives close was 15:40. Its December 7 timetable distinguishes 15:45 segment close and 15:30 expiring index contracts. The PM's requested 15:00–15:30 desk window is an observation window. The patch at the “Closing spot proxy” panel uses an arithmetic mean of captured index prints and labels it as such. No index-print-only feed can reproduce constituent auction prices, constituent weights, or trade VWAPs. Use the official published final settlement for reconciliation. NSE Clearing specifies underlying index close; for the regime effective December 7, FAQ 72 specifies constituent VWAP over the last 30 minutes of cash continuous trading. These rules also differ from an unexpired futures contract's daily settlement. [NSE October FAQ, questions 8 and 68](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/mediaattachment/2026-10/FAQs_on_Closing_Auction_Session_CAS_in_the_CM_and_changes_in_FO_Segment_ver3_20261023220345.pdf), [NSE December FAQ, questions 13 and 72](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/web/mediaattachment/2026-11/FAQs_for_CM_and_FO_FOR_CAS_2.0_20261109152618.pdf), [NSE Clearing settlement](https://www.nseclearing.in/clearing-settlement/equity-derivatives/settlement-price).
- **HV and VRP.** Code.gs:599 requires 31 completed daily closes for HV30 and uses sample standard deviation of log returns annualized with 245 sessions. Intraday it excludes today's partial daily bar, checks the latest close date, rejects duplicate/nonpositive closes, and accepts prior data up to five calendar days old. It does not prove every intervening expected session is present. The checked-in history at docs/index.html:1334 has 258 dated rows per index from 2025-11-04 through 2026-11-17, including 257 non-null HV30, 20 broker-labelled non-null IV, and 19 paired IV/HV30. Distribution and provenance checks at :1826 and :1976 use finite sourced observations. The elapsed chart range is not one year of observed daily IV or one year of paired VRP. An intraday headline compares current IV with previously completed realized volatility.
- **OI gamma.** The browser divides raw OI by 1000 on import and multiplies by 1000 once for gamma aggregation, then by S² × 0.01 / 10,000,000 to obtain rupee crore per 1% move (docs/index.html:4745 and :2627, baseline). Call/put gamma is leg-specific and no lot multiplier re-enters the arithmetic. A regression uses 10,000 units on each side, spot 25,000 and gamma difference 0.0002, returning 1.25 crore. Upstox documents the field as open interest. Neither the feed nor this audit independently observes its owners or their hedge directions. The sum assumes signed call-minus-put OI, covers eligible recorded strikes, and has no empirical dealer-position sign. Reconcile raw OI against a contract-level exchange report for the same timestamp and unit before asserting independent unit validation. [Upstox option-chain fields](https://upstox.com/developer/api-documentation/get-pc-option-chain/).
